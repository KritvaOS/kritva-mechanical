## 1. Purpose

This index lists the 24 PNG artwork sheets for the 12 feasibility concepts: one concept sheet and one cross-section sheet each. The sheets are illustrative concept artwork (evidence type C, maturity L0, PROPOSED). Values printed on them are `CONCEPT_TARGET` only: they are not requirements, not approved dimensions and not engineering evidence. The requirements register values stay `UNKNOWN` or `TBD` until the owner decides.

## 6. Owner decisions

Decided on 2026-10-31 (recorded in the READMEs and CHANGELOG CL-028):

1. DA-04 and DA-06 definitions approved as the study baseline.
2. DA-02: 8 arm actuators plus 1 optional head pan, 9 when fitted.
3. DA-04: baseline stays 10 actuators; head pan excluded unless approved.
4. DA-03 and DA-04: four wheels in two driven pairs, one drive channel per side, 2 base actuators.
5. DA-05: slip ring selected; cable loop dropped.
6. QR-06: the two actuated joints are Hip Yaw and Knee Pitch, one DOF each (decided 2026-10-31).

Still open:

1. Torso width 300 mm and adjustable camera mast on DA-01, DA-03 and DA-06 (cross-section only).
2. Body mounting interface (4 × M5, 2 × Ø5 mm dowels, indicative concept) and the height-adjustable sensor module on QR-04, QR-05 and QR-06.
3. QR-06: joint axis directions and limits, and whether the slip ring drawn at the body interface is kept.
4. Optional sensors on QR-01 and QR-02 and LiDAR on DA-03 and DA-05.
5. The variation findings F1 to F10 in `feasibility/CONCEPT_VARIATION_AUDIT.md`.

## 8. Rules

1. Artwork files are never edited silently; a change replaces the file, updates `sha256` in the manifest, resets the status to `PENDING_REVIEW` and records a CHANGELOG entry.
2. Numbers from artwork are quoted in READMEs with the source sheet named and the class `CONCEPT_TARGET`. When sheets disagree the value is `DISCREPANCY — OWNER REVIEW`.
3. Anything that cannot be read or verified from the sheet is `NOT VERIFIABLE FROM CURRENT ARTWORK`.
4. The PNG files are an input to the feasibility study only. They are not CAD, not a BOM and not a basis for procurement or fabrication.
