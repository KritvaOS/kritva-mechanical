## Pricing, data-quality and use notes

- Listing snapshots were fetched on 2026-11-28 unless the source states another date. They are not quotations, stock guarantees or purchase recommendations.
- The cube/gear actuator data use continuous torque for the continuous screen and peak only for the peak column. The 18 Nm AK10-9 is not represented as a 25–40 Nm L actuator.
- The 40 Nm T25-40 entry is third-party and explicitly unconfirmed; conclusions relying on it are low confidence.
- Imported list prices are not landed costs. Duty cannot be honestly assigned without tariff classification and shipment data; ICEGATE provides a calculator requiring those details.
- CNC hour rates and machine time are estimate inputs. The modeled hours are allowances, not supplier quotations or manufacturing instructions.
- Material density supports mass arithmetic only. No yield-strength, buckling, fatigue, weld or safety factor conclusion is derived.
- No files or values here authorize CAD, a final BOM, supplier quote, procurement, fabrication, assembly, testing or a change to D-003.
