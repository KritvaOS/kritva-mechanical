## Comparative observations (not a ranking)

- DA-01 is a fixed tabletop dual-arm concept; its stability depends on the support condition and arm pose. It has the lowest complexity label in this allocation but still has unresolved wrist and reach/pose interpretation.
- QR-02 is a four-leg, 3-DOF-per-leg concept. Workspace, ground contact, support polygon, sensor baseline and leg geometry remain unresolved.
- DA-04 adds a tall torso and differential-drive base. Ten baseline channels are eight arm joints plus two wheel-pair drive channels; the head is fixed. Battery is not shown and sizing is stated as outside the study.
- QR-04 shares the 12-joint quadruped count with QR-02 but has long-leg geometry and a cross-section-only indicative body mounting feature and adjustable sensor module. Long-leg inertia, support and dynamic loads remain unresolved.
